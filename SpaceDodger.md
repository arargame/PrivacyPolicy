# Space Dodger – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 2 September 2026**

This privacy policy explains what data the mobile game **Space Dodger** (package name `com.arargames.spacedodger`, "the Game") processes, why, and what choices you have.

The Game is developed and published by **Arar Games** ("we", "us").  
Contact: **arargame@hotmail.com**

---

## Summary

The Game itself does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, microphone, or camera access, and it does not track your precise GPS location.

However, the Game **does** show advertisements, **does** offer in-app purchases, and **does** integrate Google services. These involve data processing that is described in detail below.

---

## Data We Process

### 1. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob**.

To do this, Google may collect and process:

- Your **advertising identifier** (Android Advertising ID). The Game declares the `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission for this purpose.
- Device information such as device model, operating system version, language, and screen characteristics.
- **Approximate location derived from your IP address** (country/region level, not precise GPS location).
- Interaction data such as ad impressions and clicks.

This data is used to deliver, measure, and — where permitted — personalise ads, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud.

Google acts as an independent controller and/or processor for this data. Its handling is governed by Google's own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 2. Consent Management (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are located in the **European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland**, the Game displays a consent form provided by the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)**.

Your consent choices are stored **locally on your device** (including IAB TCF standard values). Personalised advertising is only used if you consent to it.

**You can change or fully withdraw your consent at any time** from within the Game: open **Options → PRIVACY OPTIONS**. This reopens the consent form. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

---

### 3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Game offers optional in-app purchases processed **exclusively through Google Play Billing** (e.g., removing advertisements, purchasing game currency, or unlocking items).

- All payments and transactions are processed securely by **Google Play**.
- We **never** see, receive, or store your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.
- The Game stores purchase confirmation tokens locally in its save file solely to ensure purchased items/features are correctly granted. These tokens stay on your device and are never sent to our servers.

---

### 4. Google Play Games Services

The Game integrates **Google Play Games Services** for sign-in, achievements, and cloud leaderboards.

If you choose to sign in:
- Your Play Games profile (such as gamer tag, avatar, and player ID) and submitted scores/achievements are processed by Google.
- Scores may be visible on public leaderboards according to your Google Play Games privacy settings.

Signing in is completely optional. The Game is fully playable offline and without signing in.

---

### 5. Local Game Data

Your game progress, scores, audio settings, unlocked items, and configuration are stored locally on your device (`savegame.json`).

- This file is stored in app-private storage.
- It is **not** uploaded to any external server.
- Uninstalling the Game or clearing the app data removes this local file.

---

### 6. Local Notifications

The Game may schedule **local reminder notifications** (for example, daily rewards or energy replenishment).

- These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely offline on your device.
- No personal data leaves your device for this purpose, and no remote push notification server is used.
- You can disable notifications at any time via your device's Android App Settings.

---

### 7. Crash and Performance Diagnostics

Google Play may automatically collect anonymous crash reports and performance vitals (such as ANRs and rendering rates). We use this aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic data solely to fix bugs, optimize performance, and improve game stability.

---

## Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland:

- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalised advertising and any associated access to or storage of identifiers on your device. Requested via UMP and revocable at any time.
- **Contractual necessity** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for processing and delivering in-app purchases you choose to make.
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for fraud detection, security, basic non-personalised advertising to keep the Game free, and anonymous stability monitoring.

---

## Data Retention

- **Consent choices:** Stored locally on your device until you change them, clear app data, or uninstall the Game.
- **Save data & purchase tokens:** Stored locally on your device until progress is reset or the Game is uninstalled.
- **Data held by Google:** Retained according to Google's own data retention policies.

---

## Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar data protection laws, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not operate user accounts or store identifiable personal data on external servers, third-party data is managed directly via Google:

- **Reset or delete advertising ID:** `Android Settings → Privacy → Ads`
- **Change advertising consent:** In-game via `Options → PRIVACY OPTIONS`
- **Manage Google account data:** [Google Account Privacy Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy)

For any further inquiries, contact us at **arargame@hotmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is **not directed at children under 13** (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). It serves advertising and is not part of the Google Play Designed for Families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services within the Game, please contact us at **arargame@hotmail.com** so we can take immediate action.
