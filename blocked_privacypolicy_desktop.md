# Blocked: Pixel Panzer – Privacy Policy (Windows / Desktop)

**Last Updated: 22 September 2026**

This privacy policy explains how **Blocked: Pixel Panzer** ("the Game", Windows/PC edition published on Microsoft Store and desktop platforms) handles user data.

The Game is developed and published by **Arar Games** ("we", "us").  
Contact: **arargame@hotmail.com** | **korayarar@gmail.com**

---

## 1. Summary: Zero Personal Data Collection

**Blocked: Pixel Panzer for Windows / Desktop is designed to be privacy-friendly.**

- We do **not** collect, store, share, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
- We do **not** ask for your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

---

## 2. No In-Game Advertising on Desktop

The Windows / Desktop version of Blocked contains **no third-party advertisements**:
- No advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID `AD_ID`) are collected.
- No ad network SDKs (such as AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.) are integrated into the desktop build.
- No tracking cookies, beacons, or behavioral tracking tools are embedded.

---

## 3. No Analytics or Telemetry

The desktop version of the Game does **not** include external telemetry or gameplay tracking SDKs:
- No gameplay analytics (such as Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics) are transmitted to external servers.
- Tank combat, level statistics, and gameplay occur strictly offline on your computer.

---

## 4. In-App Purchases (Microsoft Store Commerce)

If digital purchases (e.g., gold packs or tank unlocks) are made through the **Microsoft Store**:
- All payment transactions and billing details are processed securely and exclusively by **Microsoft**.
- Arar Games **never** sees, collects, or stores your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.
- Verified entitlements are stored locally in your game's encrypted local save file.

---

## 5. Local Game Data & Offline Storage

All game progression data is stored **locally on your computer**:
- **Saved Data:** Tank upgrades, level progress, gold balance, audio settings, and control bindings (`savegame.json`).
- **Storage Location:** Private local user application storage on your PC (`%LOCALAPPDATA%` / app container).
- **Data Sharing:** This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.
- **Data Deletion:** Uninstalling the Game or deleting its local data folder permanently removes all save files.

---

## 6. Platform Services (Microsoft Store)

If you obtained the Game through the **Microsoft Store**:
- Downloads, installations, updates, and any platform-level crash reporting are handled directly by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
- Arar Games does not receive or store your Microsoft account credentials or payment details.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K. Because the Game collects **zero personal information**, it does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

---

## 8. Legal Basis & Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Because Arar Games does not collect, store, or process any personal data from the desktop version of the Game, we do not maintain personal databases subject to data access or portability requests.

If you have questions regarding privacy or want clarification regarding desktop application behavior, you can contact us at any time.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this repository with an updated revision date.
