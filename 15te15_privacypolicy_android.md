# 15te15 – Privacy Policy (Android)

**Last Updated: 6 October 2026**

This privacy policy explains what data the mobile application **15te15** (package name `com.arargames.onbesteonbes`, "the App") processes, why, and what choices you have.

The App is developed and published by **Arar Games** ("we", "us").  
Contact: **arargame@hotmail.com** | **korayarar@gmail.com**

---

## Summary

The App does **not** collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera, and it does **not** track your precise GPS location.

However, the App integrates standard mobile platform services for in-app advertising, in-app purchases and subscriptions, stability and analytics telemetry, and consent management. These services involve data processing that is described in detail below.

---

## Data We Process

### 1. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

The App offers optional digital purchases and subscription plans (such as Premium membership, advanced formula filters, and ad-free experience).

- All transactions are processed **exclusively and securely through Google Play Billing**.
- All payment credentials (credit cards, banking details, billing addresses) are handled directly by Google. **Arar Games never receives, views, or stores your financial or payment details.**
- Verified purchase tokens and subscription entitlement states are cached locally on your device in secure application storage to deliver premium features offline.
- Google Play Terms of Service: [https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)

---

### 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads through **Google AdMob**.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:
- Your **Android Advertising Identifier** (Google Advertising ID / AD_ID). The App declares the `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission for this purpose.
- Technical device data (device brand and model, Android OS version, language setting, display metrics).
- **Approximate location** derived from your IP address (country/region level, never precise GPS).
- Ad interaction events, impressions, and ad engagement metrics.

This data is used to serve, measure, and — where permitted — personalise ads, as well as to detect fraud and invalid traffic.

Learn more:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 3. Analytics & Diagnostics (Google Analytics for Firebase & Crashlytics)

The App may integrate **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to monitor application health, resolve crashes, and improve coupon optimization algorithms.

Data processed includes:
- **Anonymous Usage & Event Telemetry:** Calculation triggers, formula filter activations, simulation runs, and screen views.
- **Diagnostics & Crash Logs:** Stack traces, device state at the time of an unhandled exception, OS version, and memory usage.
- **Pseudonymous Identifiers:** An auto-generated Firebase App Instance ID and anonymous installation GUID. These identifiers do not contain any personally identifiable information.

Learn more:
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

### 4. Consent Management (Google User Messaging Platform - UMP)

For users located in the **European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland**, the App implements the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to present a consent dialog on launch in compliance with GDPR and IAB TCF 2.2 standards.

- Your consent preferences are stored locally on your device.
- Personalised advertising and non-essential telemetry are activated only when permitted by your choices.
- You can review or modify your privacy choices at any time from the in-app Settings menu.

---

### 5. Local App Data & Storage

Your generated coupons, customized filters, probability settings, and calculation histories are stored **strictly on your device** in private application storage.

- We do not transmit or sell your coupon formulas or simulation results to external servers.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing application data via Android Settings permanently deletes this local data.

## Legal Basis (GDPR / KVKK)

For users protected under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Turkish KVKK (Law No. 6698):

- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):** For personalized advertising and optional analytics data collection, managed through Google UMP.
- **Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):** For fulfilling in-app purchase and subscription entitlements via Google Play Billing.
- **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** For detecting fraud, maintaining app security, diagnosing crashes, and delivering non-personalized ads.
